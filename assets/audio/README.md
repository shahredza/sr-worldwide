# Background music

Put a **royalty-free** (or your own) song here named `music.mp3`.

- A small "Play music" button then appears in the bottom-right corner of every shop page.
- It never plays by itself: visitors have to tap it.
- No file here = no button. Keep it small (under ~5 MB), and don't use copyrighted songs, since this repo is public.

Free music sources: Pixabay Music, YouTube Audio Library, Free Music Archive (check each track's licence).
